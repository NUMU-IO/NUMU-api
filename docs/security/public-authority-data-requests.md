# Policy: Requests from Public Authorities for User Data

**Owner:** يحيى شريف ابراهيم محمد — the data controller named in NUMU's privacy
policy and terms.
**Effective:** 2026-09-13
**Applies to:** every request from any government or public authority — Egyptian,
Saudi, or foreign — for data about a merchant, a merchant's customer, or a
platform user, including data NUMU receives from Meta.

NUMU holds data that people did not give us directly: a merchant's customers
hand their name, phone number and address to a store, and Meta passes us message
content and account identifiers. None of those people chose us. That is the
reason this policy exists, and the reason the default answer to an informal
request is no.

## 1. No disclosure without valid legal process

NUMU does not disclose user data to a public authority voluntarily. Disclosure
requires a written instrument that is valid under the law of the requesting
jurisdiction — a prosecutor's order, a court order, a judicial warrant, or a
statutory demand from a regulator with authority over us.

A phone call, an email, a WhatsApp message, a verbal request at a meeting, or a
request from an individual officer without an accompanying instrument is not
legal process. The answer is to ask for the written instrument.

## 2. Legality review — required before any disclosure

Every request is reviewed before a single record is released. The review
establishes and records:

- **Authenticity.** Does the instrument come from the authority it claims,
  through a channel we can verify? Where there is any doubt, we contact the
  issuing authority through publicly listed contact details, not details supplied
  in the request itself.
- **Authority.** Does the issuing body have jurisdiction over NUMU and over the
  data sought?
- **Legal basis.** Which specific law or article compels this disclosure?
- **Scope.** Is the data described with enough precision to be identified, and is
  the volume proportionate to the stated purpose?
- **Form.** Is the instrument signed, dated, and within any validity period it
  states?

If any of these cannot be established, the request is not complied with until it
is. Counsel is engaged for any request that is unclear, unusually broad, or the
first of its kind.

## 3. Challenging unlawful or overbroad requests

Where the review concludes a request is unlawful, exceeds the authority's
jurisdiction, is disproportionate in scope, or is defective in form, NUMU will
challenge it rather than comply. Challenge means, in escalating order:

1. Written objection to the issuing authority setting out the defect and asking
   that the request be withdrawn or narrowed.
2. Negotiation to reduce the scope to what is lawful and necessary.
3. Formal legal challenge through counsel, including before the courts where
   that route is available.

Compliance is not accelerated by the authority's urgency. Where a deadline makes
a proper review impossible, we ask for an extension in writing and record the
response.

## 4. Data minimisation

We disclose the minimum that the valid instrument actually compels — never the
account, never "everything you hold", never a convenience export.

- The disclosure is assembled to the specific identifiers, date ranges and record
  types named in the instrument.
- Fields the instrument does not name are withheld or redacted, including any
  data about third parties caught incidentally in the same records.
- Data obtained from Meta is disclosed only where the instrument reaches it
  specifically, and remains subject to Meta's Platform Terms.
- Where a narrower record answers the authority's stated question, we offer it in
  place of the broader one.

## 5. Documentation

Every request is logged, whether or not it results in a disclosure, in the
request register held by the owner. The register records:

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Reference | Sequential internal ID |
| Date received | And the channel it arrived through |
| Requesting authority | Body, country, and the named officer or office |
| Instrument | Type, reference number, date, issuing signatory |
| Data sought | As described in the instrument |
| Legal basis cited | Specific law or article |
| Reviewers | Everyone who took part, including external counsel |
| Legal reasoning | Why the request was judged lawful or unlawful |
| Outcome | Disclosed, partially disclosed, challenged, withdrawn, refused |
| Data disclosed | Exact fields and record counts released |
| User notified | Yes/no, with date, or the legal reason notification was barred |

The register and the underlying instruments are retained for **seven years** from
the date of the request. Entries are never deleted, including where a request is
withdrawn.

## 6. Notifying the people affected

Where the law permits it, NUMU notifies the merchant or user whose data was
sought, before disclosure if there is time and afterwards if there is not. Where
a gag provision or statutory bar prevents notification, the bar and its expiry
are recorded in the register, and notification is given once it lapses.

## 7. Emergency requests

An authority may assert an imminent risk of death or serious physical harm. Such
a request may be answered without a written instrument only where the owner
judges the risk credible and immediate, and only with the minimum data capable of
addressing that specific risk. Every emergency disclosure is logged under
section 5 within 24 hours, with the reasoning that justified it.

## 8. Review

This policy is reviewed annually by the owner, and immediately after any request
that it did not cleanly cover.
