**Why this grows your store:** shoppers who added items but didn't check out are your warmest
leads — they already wanted to buy. A timely reminder (often with a small discount) recovers a
meaningful share of that lost revenue at almost no cost.

## When to recommend
Recommend this when the store has a non-zero number of abandoned checkouts in the recent period.

## Steps to enable
1. Go to **Marketing → Abandoned Checkouts** and enable recovery.
2. Set the message and timing (for example after 1 hour and 24 hours).
3. Optionally include a discount code.
