**Why this grows your store:** customers who already bought once are far cheaper to sell to
again than acquiring new ones. WhatsApp has very high open rates in your market, so a relevant
broadcast to repeat buyers drives reliable repeat revenue.

## When to recommend
Recommend this when the store has a meaningful number of customers with more than one order.

## Steps to enable
1. Connect WhatsApp Business under **Settings → Channels → WhatsApp**.
2. Build a **repeat buyers** segment.
3. Go to **Marketing → Broadcasts → New** and send a relevant offer to that segment.
