**Why this grows your store:** if you sell items that pair naturally (or you carry slow stock),
a Buy-One-Get-One offer raises average order value and clears inventory while feeling like a
deal to the customer.

## When to recommend
Recommend this when the catalog has multiple related products or bundle-like items that pair well.

## Steps to enable
1. Go to **Marketing → Promotions → New → BOGO**.
2. Choose the trigger product(s) and the reward.
3. Set the date range and activate.
