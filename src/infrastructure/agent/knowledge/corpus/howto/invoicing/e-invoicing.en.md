NUMU can issue compliant electronic invoices and submit them to the Egyptian Tax Authority
(ETA). Saudi (ZATCA) and Fawaterak are also supported.

## Steps
1. Go to **Settings → Invoicing → E-invoicing**.
2. Choose your authority (for example **ETA Egypt**).
3. Enter your tax registration details and connect your credentials.
4. Enable automatic submission so each order's invoice is filed.
5. Save.

## Notes
You can download any invoice as a PDF from the **Orders** screen. Standard (non-tax) invoices
are available even without e-invoicing enabled.
