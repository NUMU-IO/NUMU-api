BOGO (Buy One, Get One) is a promotion where buying a qualifying product unlocks another
for free or at a discount — great for moving bundles and slow stock.

## Steps
1. Go to **Marketing → Promotions → New → BOGO**.
2. Pick the **trigger** products (what the customer must buy).
3. Pick the **reward** (the free or discounted product).
4. Set the **date range** for the campaign.
5. Save and activate.

## Notes
You can also create coupon codes and free-shipping promotions from the same Promotions
screen.
