NUMU detects carts a shopper left without checking out and can automatically send recovery
messages by email or WhatsApp to win the sale back.

## Steps
1. Go to **Marketing → Abandoned Checkouts**.
2. Enable abandoned-cart recovery.
3. Customize the **message** and the **timing** (for example, 1 hour and 24 hours after).
4. Save.

## Notes
Recovery messages can include a discount code to nudge the shopper. Track recovered revenue
in the same screen.
