NUMU connects to WhatsApp Business so you can broadcast offers and updates to customers who
opted in — a high-open-rate channel in Egypt and the Gulf.

## Steps
1. Go to **Settings → Channels → WhatsApp** and connect your WhatsApp Business number.
2. Build a customer **segment** (for example, repeat buyers).
3. Go to **Marketing → Broadcasts → New**, write your message, and pick the segment.
4. Send now or schedule it.

## Notes
Keep broadcasts relevant and infrequent to protect your sender reputation. You can reuse
segments for abandoned-cart and back-in-stock messages too.
