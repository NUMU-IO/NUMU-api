Bosta is an Egyptian courier you can connect to NUMU for automated shipping labels and
live tracking.

## Steps
1. Go to **Settings → Shipping → Bosta**.
2. Paste your Bosta **API key**.
3. Select your **pickup location**.
4. Save and enable Bosta.

## Notes
Once connected, orders can create Bosta shipments automatically, and your customers get a
tracking number. You can also connect MyLerz or Shippo the same way under **Settings →
Shipping**.
