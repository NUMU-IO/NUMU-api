Paymob lets your NUMU store accept Visa, Mastercard, and Meeza card payments online.

## Steps
1. Go to **Settings → Payments** in your Merchant Hub.
2. Choose **Paymob** from the list of payment providers.
3. Enter your Paymob **API key** and **Integration ID** (from your Paymob dashboard).
4. Save, then toggle **Paymob** on.
5. Place a small test order to confirm the checkout flow works before going live.

## Notes
You can enable more than one payment method at the same time (for example Paymob for
cards plus Cash on Delivery). Customers pick their preferred method at checkout.
