Cash on Delivery lets customers pay in cash when their order arrives — the most popular
payment method across Egypt and the MENA region.

## Steps
1. Go to **Settings → Payments → Cash on Delivery**.
2. Toggle **Cash on Delivery** on.
3. Optionally require a **partial deposit** to confirm the order.
4. Optionally set a **COD fee per governorate** to cover collection costs.
5. Save your changes.

## Notes
COD orders appear in **Orders** like any other order; mark them paid when the courier
returns the collected cash.
