Your NUMU store comes with a free subdomain, and you can connect your own custom domain
(like `mystore.com`) so customers see your brand.

## Steps
1. Go to **Settings → Domains**.
2. Click **Connect existing domain** and enter your domain.
3. Add the DNS records NUMU shows you at your domain registrar.
4. Wait for verification — NUMU issues an SSL certificate automatically.
5. Set it as your **primary domain**.

## Notes
You can also create content **pages** (About, Contact, FAQ) and edit storefront navigation
under **Online Store → Pages** and the theme editor.
