The Analytics dashboard shows how your store is performing — sales, orders, top products,
and traffic — so you can make decisions with real numbers.

## Steps
1. Go to **Analytics** in your Merchant Hub.
2. Pick a **date range** at the top.
3. Review the cards: **total sales**, **orders**, **average order value**, and **conversion**.
4. Scroll to **top products** and **sales by channel** for detail.

## Notes
Use the date comparison to see whether a campaign moved the needle. Live figures like today's
order count always reflect real-time data.
