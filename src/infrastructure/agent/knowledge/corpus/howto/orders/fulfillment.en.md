The Orders screen is where you process every sale — from new order to delivered.

## Steps
1. Go to **Orders** in your Merchant Hub.
2. Open an order to see items, customer, and shipping address.
3. Click **Fulfill** to create a shipment (with a connected courier like Bosta, a tracking
   number is generated automatically).
4. For Cash on Delivery, mark the order **paid** once the courier returns the cash.
5. Update the status as the order moves to **delivered**.

## Notes
You can filter orders by status (new, fulfilled, delivered, cancelled) and download invoices
from each order.
