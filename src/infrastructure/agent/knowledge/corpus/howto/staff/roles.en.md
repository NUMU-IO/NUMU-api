Invite team members to help run your store and control what each can see and do with roles
and permissions.

## Steps
1. Go to **Settings → Staff**.
2. Click **Invite staff** and enter their email.
3. Assign a **role** (for example Manager, or a custom role).
4. Fine-tune **permissions** for that role (products, orders, themes, and more).
5. Send the invite.

## Notes
The store owner always has full access. Permissions are enforced everywhere, including the
NUMU Agent — staff can only do through the Agent what their role already allows.
